I talk about using AI safely.

I train for the Eesti.ai initiative “The most AI-savvy nation” and for Veebikool. The audience is ordinary people who use AI at work, not developers. In the room I show real answers from real models, not slides about what might happen.

  • What to share with AI. What actually leaves your computer and where it ends up.
  • Prompt injection. When AI treats a web page, an email or a downloaded file as instructions.
  • AI agents and permissions. What an agent may do on its own and when a human has to approve first.
  • Hallucinations and checking. Why the same question gets different answers, and what to always double-check.
  • Security of vibe-coded apps. Leaked keys, missing checks, and data that breaks.

A friend of vibe coders.

I love that good ideas no longer die behind a crazy invoice. I've seen a single button cost €500 too many times. When people can build the simple stuff themselves, that's a good thing.

You

Build the business logic

You know what your business needs, and you vibe-code exactly that part.

Me

Keep the technical side solid

Security, servers, backups, and data that doesn't break when more users show up.

If you're building something for yourself, you probably don't need help. But once you're vibe-coding other people's workflows, security and data integrity suddenly matter. That's how I work with Gold Adam right now: their team vibe-codes an internal app and I keep it reliable.

If you've already built your thing and got stuck, book a call and we'll look at it together. I promise my first reaction won't be “rewrite it from scratch”.
